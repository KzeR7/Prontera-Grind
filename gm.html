Suspicious saves

The server audits every accepted save on its own — no one has to be watching the game. It breaks a flag into two kinds: impossible (a stat above the level cap, more stat points than any level can earn, an over-cap refine) and too fast (kills, levels, Zeny or materials gained since the previous sync faster than the game itself can pay out, with GM gifts and offline claims credited back). Flags are REVIEW-ONLY: nothing here rejects a save or bans an account, and a first-time flag also mails the owner in-game. Cross-check Gifts and History before acting.
