# Expanded FX review — research and concept notes

These are review-only concepts, not production game changes or exact recreations of the RO client. iRO Wiki descriptions inform skill identity; the sources do not establish exact visual frames. Existing RO flipbooks in `assets/ro-fx/` remain in the current comparison. The new concepts are custom vector animation.

* [Sorcerer](https://irowiki.org/wiki/Sorcerer): freezing winds, earth spikes, lightning spear. Diamond Dust, Earth Grave and Varetyr use those distinct reads. Poison Buster is a custom violet toxic burst.
* [Blinding Mist](https://irowiki.org/wiki/Blinding_Mist): opaque ground mist, rather than a single projectile. Dispell is a custom dispersing ward.
* [Ninja object reconstruction](https://github.com/kindraywind/rustnarok-client/pull/1064): Swirling Petal describes a ring of kunai, ribbons and falling petals. The concept uses kunai and petals; it is not a frame-exact reproduction. Thrown kunai and huuma have visible weapons; Kunai Explosion retains a red/orange impact.
* [Wanderer](https://irowiki.org/wiki/Wanderer): Severe Rainstorm is a raining arrow volley; [Minstrel](https://irowiki.org/wiki/Minstrel) describes Sheltering Bliss as a protected area. Gloomy Shyness uses a custom subdued palette, adjusted for this game's buff rather than iRO's PvP debuff.
* Additional researched identity for subsequent work: [Royal Guard](https://irowiki.org/wiki/Royal_Guard), [Geneticist](https://irowiki.org/wiki/Geneticist), [Sura](https://irowiki.org/wiki/Sura), [Shadow Chaser](https://irowiki.org/wiki/Shadow_Chaser).

## Throwing-weapon pass (review concepts)

* [iRO Wiki — Ninja](https://irowiki.org/wiki/Ninja) describes Throw Kunai as three hits; [Throw Huuma Shuriken](https://irowiki.org/wiki/Throw_Huuma_Shuriken) describes a boomerang-like weapon hitting an area. Those are the basis for the three-hit timing and the spinning out-and-return path.
* The [RO client throw reconstruction](https://github.com/akreao/roBrowserLegacy/pull/159) describes a small rotating thrown item with three trailing copies, and reports no hit effect in that client. This preview deliberately exaggerates the weapon silhouettes and adds custom ribbons, flash, cutting arcs, impact sparks and shockwaves for readability and impact. It does **not** claim those embellishments are original RO art. The storm variant is a custom escalation of this game's Huuma Shuriken Storm, not a claim about a specific official animation.

## RO-base comparison pass

The New comparison now plays the game's actual current shared effect and the current `PGSignatureFx` layer where one exists, with the concept layered on top. `PGSignatureFx` uses actual RO client flipbooks for some skills (for example Holy Cross, Grand Cross, Shield Boomerang, Shield Chain, Gloria Domini, Divestment, Kunai Explosion and Soul Cutter); other skills use the game's procedural signatures, and still others have only shared non-RO effects. The shared Afterglow effects are **not original RO client FX**, and the preview does not claim that every skill has a dedicated RO asset. New-mode thrown kunai and huuma intentionally replace the current projectile rather than playing both at once. Triggered sub-effects are shown as current effects until they receive individual concepts.

The Huuma now uses the game's existing `assets/equipment-icons/13300.png` (RO Huuma Wing) instead of the external shuriken. Its pixels are not modified. The kunai is a custom projectile with a fixed angle and a single straight screen-space line; its three throws are staggered along that same line. Existing client-asset provenance is recorded in `assets/equipment-icons/manifest.json` and `tools/native-expanded/README.md`.

## Deeper client execution research — work in progress

The previous concepts are **not** validated recreations, and simply layering them on the shared FX is not an acceptable substitute for skill-specific sequences. The following references are starting points for a rebuild; timings and version differences must not be inferred from filenames alone.

- [roBrowserLegacy PR 32](https://github.com/Flux159/roBrowserLegacy/pull/32), head `a36cc4923856b9db94173049dc07f2b67e30fb81`, adds explicit native effect mappings. Overbrand separates caster/cast-bottom and hit; Diamond Dust separates cast, effect, and bottom; Earth Grave separates effect, bottom, and hit; Severe Rainstorm separates cast and effect. Genesis Ray similarly separates cast/bottom, effect and hit. This is a reference implementation, not proof that it matches every official client's timing.
- [Official-client Ninja analysis, PR 406](https://github.com/akreao/roBrowserLegacy/pull/406): Swirling Petal places spinning huuma, blue rotating ribbons and scatter at the ground cell; Soul Cutter has a target effect at damage arrival; Kunai Explosion uses a thrown kunai and fire burst on damage. [Ninja throws PR 159](https://github.com/akreao/roBrowserLegacy/pull/159) describes an item per damage packet with trailing copies. For this project, the user's straight-line kunai requirement takes precedence over the stock hop.
- [rustnarok issue 843](https://github.com/kindraywind/rustnarok-client/issues/843) identifies native projectile texture names; the current Huuma Wing *inventory icon* is not the native projectile and has been rejected. Finding a suitable asymmetric projectile remains open.
- [rustnarok issue 782](https://github.com/kindraywind/rustnarok-client/issues/782) describes Tiger Cannon's target-centred sky-blue radial streaks on damage and Knuckle Arrow's directional blue speed lines. These are different motions, not interchangeable auras.
- [rustnarok issue 832](https://github.com/kindraywind/rustnarok-client/issues/832) documents STR playback controls and component IDs. Some effects are hardcoded primitives or sprites rather than STR files, so merely finding a texture does not reproduce their execution.
- [Ragnarok effects catalog](https://github.com/sofiaisha/ragnarok_effects_list) contains recordings of individual effect IDs, not necessarily a complete skill sequence. Examined recordings 889, 919, 927, 928, 930, 984 and 1002: they corroborate separate radial streaks, plant, ground roots, ice cloud, falling spear, spinning projectile and scatter components, respectively. These component recordings cannot establish ordering of the full skill. The catalog itself states that full skill captures are future work.
- Asset limitation: the `rAthena-Thailand/ClientROThailand` effect tree `3782f3f33626467e721aeab99f21dfaf267ed7f8` contains many corresponding `mineffect/new_*` textures but is missing most matching STR files. In particular, finding individual textures for Earth Grave, Diamond Dust, Tiger Cannon or Severe Rainstorm is not enough to reconstruct original keyframes. A few STRs exist, including Overbrand cast bottom and Rampage Blaster. The project's existing STR baker also drops empty frames and resamples, so baked timing is an approximation.

Next work: trace each requested skill's use/damage/ground hooks and visible components, replace generic stacked concepts with individually staged effects, source or make an appropriate asymmetric projectile, and visually review the resulting full set. Do not treat this research as a completed redesign.

## Class-identity correction pass

New-mode concepts no longer stack unrelated shared afterglow or signature layers. The old afterglow canvas is reset on every cast: the previous painter can leave a clip in its canvas state, causing an old effect to remain visible after switching skills. Current mode is intentionally unchanged for comparison. Triggered variants without a new concept still show current FX.

- [Monk skill descriptions](https://ratemyserver.net/index.php?page=skill_db&jid=15) identify fists/knuckles and multi-hit martial combos. Triple/Chain/Crush now use staggered blunt impacts, Glacier Fist adds ice, Palm Strike an outward pressure wave, Zen/Call Spirits use five spheres, Critical Explosion is spirit energy, Sky Net Blow a spinning-kick wake, and Rising Dragon a rising chi spiral. Existing source-informed Tiger Cannon, Rampage Blaster and Knuckle Arrow remain separate. These are custom interpretations, not verified RO frame reproductions. The game calls Glacier Fist a freezing punch even where other RO translations refer to Tiger Fist; this preview follows the game's description.
- [Moonlight Fantasy client analysis](https://github.com/akreao/roBrowserLegacy/pull/410) puts the effect on the target and describes fog/orbs, while this game describes three illusion strikes. The preview combines subdued moon/fog with three strikes and no generic purple ninja symbol. Dispell instead breaks apart a spell seal, reflecting its [buff-removal identity](https://ragnarok.fandom.com/wiki/Dispel).
- [Kunai Explosion client analysis](https://github.com/kindraywind/rustnarok-client/issues/845) specifies a kunai throw followed by the fire-burst sprite at the target. Preview uses a straight throw and the project's existing `bakuretsu.webp` without the unrelated shared green/geared effect; red sparks are custom.
- [Summon Flora](https://wiki.playragnarokzero.com/wiki/Bio_Cannibalize) is a ground plant summon, including Geographer. `tools/review-assets/geographer-idle.png` and `flora-idle.png` are 240px-frame sprite sheets, composed from ACT action 0 using the existing `tools/native-expanded/roact.py` reader. Source: `linhan85/rAthenaCN_LeeClient`, commit `bc268afe2d9686a8d61c87d1821e0c75d1b6431d`, sprite tree `5ac05acb1a81dd8d1a9992d8d98f761d1496bf5c`; `geographer.spr` blob `141a0628c14e1a14b926ce517623a56c4db15547`, `.act` `65b140061c1686f64fcc102f43e61717a1afa074`; `flora.spr` `a48ecb1688353279c818ee599d0633f98eb1fe0c`, `.act` `c006cbea0e99f69b3a6a79cd975b6ebf4baae1a6`. Client pixels are not repainted; reveal and ground effects are custom. The preview depicts the initial summon, not its full persistent field or later attacks.
- Scope remains incomplete: earlier batch concepts for other classes have not all received a similarly detailed action/weapon review, and the rejected Huuma inventory icon is still present. Do not interpret the existence of a New toggle as evidence that every skill has been researched or finalized.
