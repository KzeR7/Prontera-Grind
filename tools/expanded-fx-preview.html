About this preview

Current mode shows the shared battlefield effect and signature layer. New mode shows review concepts with selected original layers where requested. Passive skills have no cast effect and are not listed.

Ground fields and triggered effects are presented as individual casts; combat timing and repeated field ticks are not simulated.
