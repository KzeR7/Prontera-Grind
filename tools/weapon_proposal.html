Where this art comes from

Provenance: every weapon image is a real Ragnarok Online client sprite (data/sprite/아이템/*.spr and the held-weapon sprites under data/sprite/인간족/), decoded and cropped only — never redrawn, recoloured or hand-scaled. House rule 1 forbids inventing sprite art, so nothing was drawn here.
In the game: the saved defaults are composited onto the class sprites. This page is a review tool: edits here do not change the shipped defaults; copy a hand-back into assets/weapon_proposal_data.js to update what the game uses.
