How this works

1. Pick a class at the top.  The tabs are the three animations the game plays.
2. Each card is one camera view: 0 S, 1 SW, 2 W, 3 NW, 4 N — plus the three mirrored ones.
3. Click a card, then either
     • press a whole-cycle button  → fills all 8 walk frames (or 6 attack frames) at once, or
     • click one pose under “single poses” → fills the frame you are on, then moves to the next.
4. The preview shows the real thing: pose + hair on that pose's measured head seat.
   On the Attack tab the weapon is the game's own item icon — drag it in the preview to move it.
5. “Fill from sheet” does step 3 for every view using the sheet's own layout; then you only
   fix what looks wrong.  Empty frames (dashed) keep the previous frame.
