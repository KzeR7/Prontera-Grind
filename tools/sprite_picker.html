Choose the attack poses. Click a view, then click the picture you want: it fills that frame and steps to the next one. Click the same picture again to repeat it, and leaving a frame empty is fine — it holds the pose before it. Every figure in the sheet is offered, even ones that are not part of an attack set.
Heads: the head is drawn in every picture and you can seat it by hand — drag anywhere in a picture; the blue ring is the spot that moves, and the shaded box on the selected view is the head's own pixels. This is per frame; “use this head on all 6 frames” copies it.
S = he faces you (front). N = he faces away (back) — he walks up the screen as N, so that is where you see his back. The weapon is not part of this page: it is parked until the poses are confirmed.
