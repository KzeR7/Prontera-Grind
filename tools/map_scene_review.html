Surface detail & clearance

  • Continuous Astral paving; irregular fractured volcanic ground.
  • Branching cracks, chipped edges and worn grain.
  • Actual loose bricks and rock piles outside combat bounds.
  • Crusted lava, overflow pools, falls and cooled spills.
  • Flat decorative cracks and cooled spills are not new damage hazards.

Progression & material economy

Recommended Base Lv 140–150 for Astral, Lv 150 for Thor. Ten normal stages per destination, grouped into four designs.

Provisional rates: crafting materials 0.035%, ordinary-mob sigils 0.0025%, MVP sigils 0.1%. None are guaranteed; live drops are unchanged.

Glyph crafting, boss summoning, Mythic gear and Ascension stats remain later milestones.

What remains separate

This is a scenery proposal, not a combat build. Native monster animation is still blocked on authorised ACT/SPR source files. No generic monster substitutions are hidden in these captures.

Final native-sprite readability, physical-device performance and production gameplay integration still require validation.
