How this reads

Every skill fires an animated sprite effect built the way the original client builds its .str effects: several texture layers on their own timelines, blended additively. The sheets re-render the classic effect vocabulary — pok bursts, rings, lens glows, slash streaks, ice shards, bolts, holy pillars, meteors, poison bubbles, zeny coins, the falcon. The mage line now strikes from the sky.

The hero is the game's own updated class skin — the animated APNG art with the saved weapon sprite, decoded by the game's own decoder — and what plays on the field is the game's own effect code, lifted out of index.html at load time.
