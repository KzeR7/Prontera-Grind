🧭 Spawn system — today vs two proposals

What this page is. The real play area (x −13…13, z −28…12, 26×40 units) with the real water bands of the chosen map, animated at the real constants. A simulated hero walks to the nearest monster and kills at your chosen rate; the three panels are the three spawn systems. Watch the numbers under each panel — “mobs within 10” is the honest answer to “the map feels empty”, and “walk per 10 kills” is how much of the field you never see. Payon stage 1 has a creek in the middle of the lane and Izlude has a shoreline, so switch maps to see how each system handles water.

1 · Today — 3 clumped packs, wave respawn

Three sites rolled inside a 18×24 box in the middle of the lane; 2–3 mobs of one species within ±0.7 units of the *same point*; the whole field is thrown away and re-rolled 0.7s after the last mob dies.

2 · Proposal A — camps + scatter ★

8–12 camps over the whole lane (min 5.5 apart); 1–3 mobs per camp spaced 1.6–3.2 units; each camp refills on its own 4–8s timer; idle mobs wander around their camp.

3 · Proposal B — individual spawn points

No camps at all: ~18 single-monster spawn points, each with its own 5–9s respawn timer, scattered the same way. More RO-honest, but pets, AoE and target routing lose their pack grouping.
