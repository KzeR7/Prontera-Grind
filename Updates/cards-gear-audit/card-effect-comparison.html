How to read the proposed values

Nightmare keeps the proposed mechanic with 25% stronger positive numeric bonuses. Trigger counts, durations, cooldowns and penalties stay fixed. Peco Peco boosts attack speed against ordinary monsters; Zombie reduces their incoming damage without healing. Elder Willow boosts area magic without a movement condition. Ambernite adds flat MDEF (matching Wraith’s amount, as requested); its existing slots are retained. All equipment/card movement still shares the 20% cap. Conditional effects need implementation and balance testing before release.

Crit, attack-speed, movement and healing caps still apply. Triggered damage cannot trigger more card effects. Shields refresh instead of stacking. Scorpion supplies its own poison; it does not require an existing poison skill. Existing cards should use explicit conversion rather than silently changing equipped builds.

DEF/MDEF penetration affects only the stated hit. Healing/EXP/loot and pet bonuses need online/offline parity checks. Percentage drop bonuses multiply the base chance; they are not percentage points.
