The ÷3 is a flat multiplier on every drop's value, so it hits level 1 and level 150 equally — unlike the audit's earlier "level term .15 → .10" idea, which only bit at the top. Handing power back through affixes (+30% magnitude) and cards (+30% values) lands the endgame at ×0.48 — i.e. a 52% nerf, not a 72% one, and mobs still die in under a third of a second. Max HP is the one casualty: the flat values on headgear (×4) and accessories (×3) are the HP, so dividing those two slots cuts max HP to 36%. Affix/card give-backs do not restore HP (60,099 → 60,442). That is why the HP-slot question below defaults to keep them.
