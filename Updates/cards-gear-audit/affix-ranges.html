Random gear affix ranges

Exact per-affix value ranges used by the current game build. The table shows what one random affix can add to a weapon or other equipped gear.

Values are per affixNot the sum of an item's affixes, refinements or cards.

How the range is calculated

raw = max(1, round((1 + section) × AM[rarity] × AB[affix] × rnd(0.80, 1.25)))
Crit DMG only: value = max(1, round(raw × 0.70)) · all other affixes: value = raw

The random multiplier is uniform from 0.80 up to (but not including) 1.25. The base result is rounded to the nearest integer, then floored at +1. Gear Crit DMG alone receives a second 70% post-roll scale and is rounded again (for example, 100 becomes 70); every other affix is unchanged. It is also the one affix that does not roll everywhere: since v73 it appears on weapons and accessories only. Card Crit DMG values are unchanged — they use their own 3/7/10/13 ladder rather than the AB weight, and cards can no longer be mistaken for gear rolls. Reading the values

  • AB weight is the live per-affix weight from the game. For example, Max HP has weight 12, while Crit has weight 0.32.
  • STR, AGI, DEX, LUK and INT are attribute points; Max HP is flat HP; ATK, Crit, ASPD, Flee and Crit DMG are percentage points as applied by the game.
  • An item rolls 1 Common, 1–2 Fine, 2 Rare, 2–3 Epic, or 3 Legendary affixes. It samples distinct affixes; it does not receive every row in its table.
  • Gear Crit DMG is rounded as before, then scaled to 70% of that rolled value; a value of 100 becomes 70. This post-roll adjustment applies only to gear affixes: card Crit DMG values and all other affixes are unchanged.
  • Crit DMG is a weapon/accessory affix (v73). Armor, headgear, shield and legwear never roll it, while weapons and the two accessory slots still do — so at most three worn pieces can carry the affix at once.
  • Weapon and armor/equipment slot do not change affix magnitude. Card effects are not included in these ranges.
