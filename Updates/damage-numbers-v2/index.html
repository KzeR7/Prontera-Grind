1. The fight — your settings, live

Every swing prints one number per monster (as shipped). The dashed lines mark how far your numbers travel: gold is the top of the climb, white is where the fade starts. The dots are the flight path, so you can see the difference between two settings without having to remember them.

2. Size — the first of the two things you wanted to change

Normal hit — 17px (the game today)
Skill hit — 17px (the game today)
Critical hit — 32px (the game today)

How big is each step, really? The digits are drawn at the size you pick, and the crit's starburst is built from that size — a bigger crit size makes a bigger star, not just bigger numbers.

Today: normal and skill are both 17px (the colour is what tells them apart) and a crit is 32px, which is nearly double. If 24px feels right for the crit you may also want the normal hits at 20–22px so the crit still stands out.

3. How high it flies — the other thing you wanted to change

The number climbs, settles at the top and fades there. Today the climb is 30px — about one monster-head above the head. Dragging this moves the gold line on the fight above, so you can judge it against the monster rather than against a description.

Climb — 30px above the head

Long numbers and short numbers travel the same distance — the climb is a distance in pixels, not a multiple of the number's size, so it never drifts off the screen on a boss fight.
